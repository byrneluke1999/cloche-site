Delete your Cloche account

Cloche is made by Luke Byrne. This page explains how to delete your Cloche account and the data in it.

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Cloche and tap You.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and data are deleted straight away.

Without the app

If you can't open the app, or you've uninstalled it, email help@cloche.one from the email address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it within 7 days and email you to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in record (your email address).
  • Every plant in your greenhouse, with its history, notes and watering schedule.
  • All the photos you've added.
  • Your reminder settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Our database provider's routine backups still hold a copy until they're overwritten, within 30 days.
  • Pl@ntNet, which identifies plants from photos, keeps its own record of identification requests under its own policy.
  • Crash reports in our error-tracking tool carry an account ID but no email, photos or plant names. Once your account is deleted, that ID no longer points to anyone.

Deleting your account can't be undone. For more, see the privacy policy.
