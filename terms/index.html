Plant identification and care advice

Cloche suggests what a plant is from a photo, and suggests how to care for it, using third-party services and an AI model. These suggestions can be wrong. In particular:

  • Identification is a best guess. Check the species before you rely on it.
  • Care advice is a starting point. Watering intervals, light and humidity notes are typical values for a species, not instructions for your plant in your home. Change them if your plant tells you otherwise.
  • Toxicity. Where Cloche says a plant is toxic to animals, that comes from a trusted source and has been checked. Where it says nothing, that does not mean the plant is safe. If you have pets or young children, check any plant with a vet, a doctor or a trusted source before bringing it home. If you think a person or animal has eaten something harmful, contact a doctor, a vet or a poisons information service straight away.
  • Pot labels from garden centres open a plant with Cloche's care details. The shop provides the species; the care details are Cloche's, not the shop's.

Cloche is not professional horticultural, veterinary or medical advice.
