Cloche is your digital greenhouse. It knows who each of your plants are, what they need and treats them as individuals.

How it works

Four steps. No green thumb needed.

  1. Step 1

    Add a plant

    Take a photo and Cloche suggests what it is. You confirm the right one, or type the name yourself.

  2. Step 2

    Get the care

    See how much light and water it likes, with a sensible watering schedule filled in. Change it to suit your home.

  3. Step 3

    Get a nudge

    Cloche reminds you when a plant is thirsty. Tap once to say you've watered it.

  4. Step 4

    Watch it grow

    Add photos over time and see each plant's history, from the day you brought it home.

For garden centres and plant shops

Send every plant home with a plan.

We're working with independent garden centres and plant shops in Dublin on a free spring pilot. Put a small Cloche label on the pots you choose. A customer scans it and the plant lands in their Cloche with the species and care already filled in, so they leave knowing how to keep it alive.
