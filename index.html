For plant lovers

Four steps. No green thumb needed.

Cloche works out what each of your plants is, how to look after it and when it needs water, then keeps its story as it grows.

  1. Step 1

    Add a plant

    Take a photo and Cloche suggests what it is. You confirm the right one, or type the name yourself.

  2. Step 2

    Get the care

    See how much light and water it likes, with a sensible watering schedule filled in. Change it to suit your home.

  3. Step 3

    Get a nudge

    Cloche reminds you when a plant needs water, a feed or a bigger pot. Tap once when it's done.

  4. Step 4

    Watch it grow

    Add photos over time, each lined up with the last, and see every plant's history from the day you brought it home.

Your greenhouse

Every plant on its own shelf.

Each plant's page

Everything it likes, at a glance.

Open a plant and its care is laid out in simple tiles: when it next needs water and food, how much light it wants, where it's happiest, what soil to use and when it'll want a bigger pot. If it's not safe for pets, it says so.

New in Cloche

More than watering.

The latest version looks after the rest of your plant's life too.

  • Feeding, in season

    Each plant gets a feeding plan for its growing season, and a nudge on the days it's due.

  • Time for a bigger pot?

    When a plant has likely outgrown its pot, Cloche suggests a repot. Gently, and only once.

  • Photos that line up

    Your last photo shows faintly in the camera, so every new one matches. Bit dark in there? Cloche will say.

  • Today, swiped

    Each job is a tile in its care colour. Swipe it done, or put it off till later.

  • Find it fast

    Filter your greenhouse by herbs, succulents, flowering plants and more, and swipe between indoors and outdoors.

  • Your data, yours

    Download everything Cloche keeps about you, photos included, whenever you like.
