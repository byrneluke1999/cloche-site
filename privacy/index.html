Cloche privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

In short. Cloche keeps a private inventory of your plants. We collect your email, your plants and your plant photos so the app can work, and nothing else. We don't sell your data, we don't show adverts, and we don't track you across other apps. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Cloche is run by Luke Byrne, an individual based in Ireland ("we", "us"). We are the controller of your personal data. You can reach us at help@cloche.one.

Where that happens, we rely on recognised safeguards for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses.

Plant identification and AI

When you take or choose a photo to identify a plant, the photo is sent to Pl@ntNet, a third-party plant recognition service, and to nobody else. It returns a few likely species and you confirm the right one. Identification is a guess and can be wrong, so always check before relying on it.

When you confirm a species, we ask an AI model (Claude, by Anthropic) for typical care advice for that species. Only the species name is sent. The advice is a suggested starting point that you can change, not professional horticultural advice.

Notifications

Cloche's reminders are scheduled on your phone itself. We don't use a server to push notifications, and no notification token is stored. You can switch reminders off in the app (You, then Reminders) or in your phone's settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the app (You, then Account, then Delete account), we permanently delete your plants, photos, reminder settings and your sign-in record straight away. Copies in the database provider's routine backups are overwritten on its normal backup cycle, within 30 days. Pl@ntNet keeps its own record of identification requests under its own policy.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data;
  • correct anything that's wrong (you can also edit plants yourself in the app);
  • delete your data (you can do this yourself in the app);
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • move it to another service.

Email us at the address above and we'll respond within one month. If you're not happy with how we've handled your data you can complain to your data protection authority: in Ireland, the Data Protection Commission; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections. Your account is protected by a one-time code sent to your email, and your photos are in a private store that only your account can read. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable care to protect what you give us.

Children

Cloche is not aimed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or who we share it with, we'll update this page and the date at the top, and tell you in the app if the change matters.
