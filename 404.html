Add this plant to Cloche

This label is for Cloche, a digital greenhouse that tells you when your plants need water.

  1. Open Cloche on your phone.
  2. Tap Add a plant, then Scan a pot label.
  3. Point it at the code on the pot, or type the code above.

Don’t have the app yet? Cloche is in a small private beta. Ask in the shop, or write to help@cloche.one.
